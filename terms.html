A personal project

Natasha is an AI assistant operated by Benjamin for his own Gmail account using OpenClaw hosted by MyClaw. It is not a public email service or an invitation for other people to connect their accounts. This website provides information about the project.

Account access and instructions

Google account access is granted through Google OAuth and is limited by the permissions approved by the account owner. The owner is responsible for the assistant’s configuration, instructions and any scheduled actions, and for using the integration only with information they are entitled to access.

AI output and email actions

AI-generated summaries, suggested replies and other outputs can contain errors or omit context. The owner should check important information and review consequential actions, including sending, changing or removing messages.

Availability and third-party services

This personal project may change, pause or stop. Its operation depends on Google, MyClaw, OpenClaw and the configured AI services. Their applicable terms and policies continue to govern their own services.

Privacy and ending access

The privacy policy describes how the integration handles Google user data. The owner can stop using Natasha and revoke the Google connection at any time. Revocation does not automatically remove data already stored in other services.
