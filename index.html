Natasha is Benjamin’s private AI assistant, running on OpenClaw through MyClaw. She connects to his own Gmail account to help him work with email.

What Natasha is for

Natasha supports personal email tasks, such as finding relevant messages, summarising email and helping prepare replies. Available actions depend on the Gmail permissions granted and the instructions configured by her owner.

Who can use her

This integration is for Benjamin’s own Gmail account. It is not offered as a public service, and this website does not provide account registration or access to the assistant.

How email information is handled. Gmail information used in a task may be processed by MyClaw and the configured AI service. Read the privacy policy for access, processing, storage and removal details, and the terms of use for the scope of this personal project.
